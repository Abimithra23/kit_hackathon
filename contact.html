Frequently Asked Questions

How do I enroll in a course?

To enroll in a course, browse our course catalog, select your desired course, and click the "Enroll Now" button. You'll need to create an account or log in, then complete the payment process.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers. Payment plans are available for certain courses.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, we offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied with a course, you can request a full refund within 30 days of enrollment.

Are the courses self-paced?

Most of our courses are self-paced, allowing you to learn at your own schedule. Some courses have recommended completion timelines and weekly milestones to keep you on track.

Do I get a certificate upon completion?

Yes! Upon successfully completing a course, you'll receive a verified certificate that you can share on LinkedIn, your resume, and other professional platforms.

How long do I have access to course materials?

You have lifetime access to all course materials, including videos, resources, and updates. You can revisit the content anytime after enrollment.
